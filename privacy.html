Valuegram Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Valuegram app collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. Valuegram is operated by Throne network , kolkata, INDIA. By creating an account you agree to this policy. You must be 18 or older to use Valuegram. See also our Terms of Service.

1. What we collect

  • Account data: your email address, username and password. Your password is stored in protected (hashed) form and we cannot read it.
  • Profile data (optional): display name, bio and profile photo.
  • Content you create: photos, videos, captions, comments and the category you choose for a post.
  • Activity: who you follow, your likes, saved posts, blocks, and reports you send.
  • Technical data: basic logs kept by our hosting providers (such as IP address and request times), used for security and to keep the service running.

We do not collect your location or contacts. The app only reads photos or videos that you choose to upload. We do not show ads, and we do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

2. How we use it

  • to create and run your account and show your profile and posts;
  • to send you emails such as sign-up confirmation and password-reset codes;
  • to keep Valuegram safe: reviewing reports, removing harmful content, and preventing spam and abuse;
  • to fix problems and improve the app;
  • to comply with the law and respond to valid legal requests.

We do not sell your personal data.

3. What other people can see

Your username, profile photo, name, bio, posts, videos, comments, like counts, and follower and following lists are visible to other Valuegram users. Your email address, saved posts, blocks and reports are private to you and us. Content you send outside the app (for example with the Share button) can be seen by whoever you send it to.

4. Who processes your data

We use these service providers, who process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: database, authentication and file storage (servers in [Aisa]).
  • Brevo: sending confirmation and password-reset emails. It may keep delivery logs for a limited time.

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own. We may also share data when the law requires it or to protect people’s safety.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account we delete your profile, posts, videos, comments, likes, follows, saves and blocks. Copies may remain in our hosting provider’s routine backups for a short time before they are overwritten. We may keep limited records where needed for safety, to enforce our rules or to comply with the law.

6. Delete your account and data

In the Valuegram app: open Profile, tap the settings icon, choose Delete account, and type DELETE to confirm. Your account and data are deleted straight away.

Without the app: email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the email address on your account with the subject “Delete my Valuegram account” and include your username. We will confirm your request and complete the deletion within 30 days, usually much sooner. What gets deleted is described in section 5.

7. Your rights and choices

You can view and edit your profile in the app, delete your posts and comments, block users, and delete your account. You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to withdraw your consent (which may mean we can no longer provide your account). We will respond within a reasonable time and as the law requires.

8. Children

Valuegram is only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think a child has an account, email us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical measures, including encrypted connections and access controls, to protect your data. No online service is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we make important changes we will tell you in the app or by email.

11. Contact and grievances

Questions, requests or complaints about your data: [Thronenetwork@proton.me]. We will acknowledge complaints promptly and resolve them within the time required by law.
